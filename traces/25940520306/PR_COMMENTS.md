## Rationale

Human myeloid and plasmacytoid dendritic cells are consistently defined as CD14-negative in standard flow cytometry gating strategies. This negative marker is critical for distinguishing these DC populations from monocytes, which are CD14-positive. The five OMIP (Optimized Multicolor Immunofluorescence Panel) references provide standardized protocols that document this consistent phenotype.

As correctly noted by @Caroline-99 in the issue discussion, the CD14-negative annotation was intentionally applied only to these two specific DC subtypes and not to the general "dendritic cell, human" term (CL_0001056), since some tissue-specific DC subsets (e.g., dermal DCs) may express CD14.
