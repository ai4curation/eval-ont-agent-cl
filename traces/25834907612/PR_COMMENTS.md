## Summary

Adds a new cell type `fibrochondrocyte progenitor cell` (FCP) under the temporary CL ID range (CL:9900000). The term represents a mesenchymal progenitor cell located in fibrocartilaginous tissues that co-expresses fibrochondrocyte and mesenchymal stem cell markers, and that gives rise to mature fibrochondrocytes and other meniscal cell types.
