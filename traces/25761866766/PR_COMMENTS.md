3. **CL_9900003: Periductal fibroblast of salivary gland**

   - References: PMID:39346911, PMID:31213547, PMID:41411773
   - Relationships: part_of UBERON:0001044 (saliva-secreting gland), capable_of GO:0030198 (extracellular matrix organization)

4. **CL_9900004: Junctional epithelial cell**
   - Parent: CL_0002077 (ecto-epithelial cell)
   - Comprehensive definition covering morphology, markers (CK19, ODAM), immune functions, and cell turnover
   - References: PMID:39590534, PMID:40723411, PMID:41331699, PMID:41143768
   - Relationships: part_of UBERON:0001949 (gingival epithelium), capable_of GO:0002227 (innate immune response in mucosa)
   - Multiple synonyms including "JE cell" and "DAT cell" with precise synonym typing

5. **CL_9900005: Tuft cell of parotid gland**
   - Parent: CL_0002204 (tuft cell)
   - Definition highlights POU2F3 expression and ductal localization
   - References: PMID:38358561, PMID:23209333, PMID:35993302, PMID:38098741
   - Relationships: part_of UBERON:0001831 (parotid gland)

6. **CL_9900006: Tuft cell of sublingual gland**
   - Parent: CL_0002204 (tuft cell)
   - Definition covers ductal localization and chemosensory/immune functions
   - References: PMID:38358561, PMID:23209333
   - Relationships: part_of UBERON:0001832 (sublingual gland)

7. **CL_9900007: Ionocyte of salivary gland**
   - Parent: CL_0005006 (ionocyte)
   - Definition emphasizes FOXI1 expression and osmotic regulation role
   - References: PMID:38358561, PMID:41721487, PMID:39346911, PMID:30069044, PMID:30069046
   - Relationships: part_of UBERON:0001044 (saliva-secreting gland), capable_of GO:0050801 (monoatomic ion homeostasis)

8. **CL_9900008: Myoepithelial cell of salivary gland**
   - Parent: CL_0000185 (myoepithelial cell)
   - Comprehensive definition covering morphology, markers (α-SMA, p63, AQP1), contractility, FGF7 production, and regenerative capacity
   - References: PMID:23209333, PMID:39346911, PMID:40837863, PMID:41271704
   - Relationships: part_of UBERON:0001044 (saliva-secreting gland), capable_of GO:0006939 (smooth muscle contraction)
