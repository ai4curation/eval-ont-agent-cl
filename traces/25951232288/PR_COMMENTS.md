## Rationale

Human dendritic cell populations (myeloid DCs and plasmacytoid DCs) are consistently gated as CD14-negative in flow cytometry analysis to distinguish them from monocytes and other myeloid cells. While some tissue-specific DC subsets (e.g., dermal DCs, which can be CD14-positive) exist, the general human DC populations are defined as CD14-negative. The OMIP references demonstrate this is the standard approach in the field.
