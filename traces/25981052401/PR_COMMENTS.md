## Rationale

1. **Flow cytometry standard practice**: The OMIP (Optimized Multicolor Immunofluorescence Panel) references demonstrate that human dendritic cells are consistently gated as CD14-negative to distinguish them from monocytes in flow cytometry.

2. **Scope limited to specific terms**: Following @Caroline-99's guidance in the issue comments, I added this annotation only to CL_0001057 and CL_0001058, NOT to the general "dendritic cell, human" term (CL_0001056), since some tissue-specific DC subsets may express CD14.

3. **Consistency with existing ontology patterns**: The `lacks_plasma_membrane_part` relation (CL_4030046) is already used extensively in the ontology for expressing negative markers (e.g., in CL_0001058 for CD11c-negative).

4. **Protein Ontology reference**: PR_000001889 represents CD14 in the Protein Ontology (PRO), as confirmed by examining related terms like CL_0001055 (CD14-positive, CD16-low monocyte).
