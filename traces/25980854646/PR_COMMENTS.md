## Rationale

This change aligns with the standard flow cytometry gating strategy described in OMIP papers, where:
- Human myeloid DCs and plasmacytoid DCs are consistently identified as CD14-negative
- This distinguishes them from monocytes, which are CD14-positive
- The CD14-negative marker is a critical discriminator in conventional DC vs monocyte differentiation
