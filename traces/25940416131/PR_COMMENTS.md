## Rationale

The issue reporter is mapping cell-type strings extracted from sample metadata (by an LLM) to the Cell Ontology, and the common abbreviations PBMC, WBC, and RPE were failing to resolve because they were not registered as synonyms.

- **PBMC** and **WBC**: explicitly confirmed by @addiehl as appropriate to add as exact synonyms.
- **RPE**: confirmed by @scheuerm as appropriate for `retinal pigment epithelial cell`. I chose `hasExactSynonym` because "RPE" is a direct initialism for "Retinal Pigment Epithelial (cell)" / "Retinal Pigment Epithelium" — the abbreviation refers to the same concept and is widely used interchangeably with the full name in the literature.

## Modeling notes

I used the OMO `abbreviation` synonym type (OMO:0003000) to flag these as abbreviations specifically, consistent with existing CL usage (e.g. HSC on CL:0000037, BFU-E/CFU-E on CL:0000038, D2-MSN on CL:4023029, DPSC on CL:4030035). The scope (`hasExactSynonym`) is orthogonal to the synonym type and indicates that the abbreviation and the term label denote the same concept.
