The original requester was mapping LLM-extracted cell-type strings from experimental sample metadata to CL and observed that common abbreviations (PBMC, WBC, RPE) failed to map because they were not registered as synonyms. Adding them as synonyms makes these strings findable by standard synonym-based lookup.

`RPE` scope: chosen as `exact` for consistency with PBMC/WBC and the abbreviation pattern. While "RPE" can also refer to the retinal pigment epithelium tissue (UBERON), in the cell-typing context covered by this CL term it functions as a direct abbreviation. The OMO abbreviation synonym type makes the abbreviation nature explicit for downstream consumers who want to disambiguate.
