## Scope decision

Per @Caroline-99's comment on the issue, the change is intentionally
**scoped to the two human-specific subset terms** (CL_0001057, CL_0001058).
The general parent term **CL_0001056 (dendritic cell, human) was left
untouched**, because some human DC subsets (e.g. dermal DCs) do express
CD14 — PMID:23621371, PMID:34279540. Asserting CD14-negativity at the
parent level would create inconsistencies with those subsets.
