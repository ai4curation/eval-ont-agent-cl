## Rationale

These human dendritic cell populations are consistently defined as CD14-negative in standard flow cytometry gating strategies to distinguish them from CD14-positive monocytes. The OMIP (Optimized Multicolor Immunofluorescence Panel) references provide authoritative support for this gating strategy.

Following the guidance from @Caroline-99 in the issue comments, I did **not** add CD14-negative to the general term CL_0001056 (dendritic cell, human), as some tissue-specific human DC subsets (e.g., dermal DCs) can express CD14.
