## Rationale

The issue (cell-ontology #3163) reports that these two human-specific dendritic cell populations are consistently gated as CD14-negative in flow cytometry, both to distinguish them from monocytes and as the standard convention across multiple OMIP publications. The five cited OMIPs (77, 87, 94, 101, 112) all use this gating strategy.

Per @Caroline-99's comment on the issue, the CD14-negative assertion was **not** added to the general human dendritic cell term CL_0001056. Some tissue-resident DC subsets (notably dermal DCs) are CD14-positive, so a blanket `lacks CD14` at the genus level would be over-asserting. The change is therefore confined to the two specific subtypes (myeloid and plasmacytoid) where the assertion is supported by the literature.

## Editorial choices

- **Relation used:** `cl:lacks_plasma_membrane_part` (`obo:CL_4030046`), consistent with how CD11c-negative is already encoded on CL_0001058 and how negative markers are recorded throughout CL (see `docs/relations_guide.md` §"cell surface (protein) markers").
- **CD14 protein identifier:** `obo:PR_000001889`, which is already used as the CD14 PRO identifier elsewhere in cl-edit.owl (e.g., the hematopoietic stem cell `lin-negative` lacks set on CL_0000037).
