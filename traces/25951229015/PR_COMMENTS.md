## Changes Made

### 1. Revised Textual Definition
- **Old definition**: "Chondrocyte that is terminally differentiated, produces type X collagen, is large in size, and often associated with the replacement of cartilage by bone (endochondral ossification)."
- **New definition**: "A chondrocyte that is part of the hypertrophic cartilage zone. This cell is significantly enlarged and characterised by high expression of type X collagen (COL10A1) in both humans and mice.
