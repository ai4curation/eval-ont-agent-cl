## Rationale
Human myeloid and plasmacytoid dendritic cells are consistently defined as CD14-negative in flow cytometry gating strategies. This negative marker is crucial for distinguishing these DC populations from monocytes, which typically express CD14. The provided OMIP (Optimized Multicolor Immunofluorescence Panel) references demonstrate standardized gating strategies that consistently gate these DC populations as CD14⁻.

As noted in the issue discussion, this change is specific to CL_0001057 and CL_0001058, and intentionally does not include the general human dendritic cell term (CL_0001056), since some tissue-specific DC subsets (e.g., dermal DCs) may express CD14.
